Co-first author of a work on context engineering, now available as a preprint on **arXiv**. The study explores how structured context design affects reasoning and decision-making in large language models, with an emphasis on theoretical understanding and reproducible empirical protocols.

**My contribution** focuses on the initial problem formulation, validation of the core idea, and the large-scale empirical design. I also propose a theoretical framing of how context structure influences model behavior, to be developed in future work.

**Keywords / skills involved:** empirical protocol design, LLM evaluation, reproducible experiments.
