First author of a double-blind submission targeting **ICLR 2027**, on expected policy gradients through hard events (guards, impacts, contact-mode switches) in differentiable simulation and model-based RL. The work combines certified handling of moving, policy-dependent event boundaries with structure-exploiting probabilistic inference, so that automatic differentiation is applied only to checked, scope-aware programs.

**My contribution** covers the method design, the formal analysis, and the experimental evaluation. Specifics are kept anonymous while the paper is under review.

**Keywords / skills involved:** differentiable simulation, hard events, measure transport, variable elimination, automatic differentiation, policy gradients.
